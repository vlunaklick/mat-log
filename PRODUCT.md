# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person who trains Brazilian jiu-jitsu (gi and no-gi) and is also the app's author. Two contexts:

- **Phone, right after class.** In the locker room or on the way home, tired, sweaty hands, a few minutes at most. Wants to dump what happened (text or audio) before it's forgotten and move on.
- **Desktop, planning.** Calm, seated, deliberate. Builds the gi/no-gi gameplan with the coach, reviews technique notes and progress.

## Product Purpose

Mat Log is a personal BJJ training journal with an AI coach. The core loop: after class, tell the coach what happened; it turns that into an editable class draft; confirm it; the session, techniques and progress update themselves. Around that loop live the technique library with spaced-repetition review, separate gi/no-gi gameplans, goals and progress charts.

Success is logging a class in under a minute on a phone, and opening the app before class to see exactly one thing to focus on.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, quiet. A notebook that happens to be smart. Voice is Rioplatense Spanish with voseo, short and direct, like a training partner rather than a product. References: Things and Apple Notes (calm, little text, things appear when needed) and Linear and Raycast (precise, fast, dense where it counts).

## Anti-references

- Fitness-tracker energy (Strava, Whoop): loud metrics, streaks, badges, motivational copy.
- Chat-app-first layouts where a history list dominates the screen.
- SaaS dashboards: hero metrics, identical card grids, explanatory paragraphs under every heading.
- Everything visible at once: secondary lists, filters and help text shown permanently instead of on demand.

## Design Principles

1. **Capture first.** The fastest path on any screen leads to logging a class. Nothing competes with it.
2. **Show on demand.** History, filters, secondary actions and explanations stay one tap away, not on the canvas.
3. **One focus per screen.** Each screen answers one question; everything else is quieter or hidden.
4. **Say it once, say it short.** No leads that restate titles, no repeated labels, no filler.
5. **The data is the color.** Chrome stays silent so the user's notes, rolls and progress carry the screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Touch targets at least 44px (used with tired hands on a phone). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Never rely on color alone for state.
